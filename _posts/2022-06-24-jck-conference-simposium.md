## Abstract

**Background and Necessity**

The gig economy has experienced exponential growth since 2010, yet platform workers remain in a blind spot regarding health rights and social safety nets. Specifically, "Replacement Drivers" (Daeri drivers) in Korea face severe health risks due to night shifts, emotional labor, and irregular working patterns. However, they are excluded from traditional workplace-based health systems, resulting in a significantly lower health check-up rate (32-50%) compared to standard employees (70-80%).

**Purpose**

This presentation proposes a "Smart Health Management" model for platform workers. It aims to build a sustainable ecosystem where workers can proactively manage their health by integrating fragmented health data (Integration) and establishing solidarity through mutual aid organizations (Solidarity).

**Key Contents**

- **The Connectivity Paradox**: Workers are hyper-connected via algorithms but isolated in reality, lacking social protection.
- **Data Integration & Structuring**: Combining national health records, life-logs (Fitbit), and psychological surveys into a precise health map.
- **Solidarity via Mutual Aid**: Establishing a health safety net led by the "Platform Workers Mutual Aid Association" rather than external researchers.
- **Accessibility & Return of Information**: Converting complex medical results into personalized "Video Prescriptions" for easy understanding.
- **On-site Healthcare (O2O)**: Utilizing night shuttles and resting shelters as mobile health examination centers.

**Expected Effect**

The data-driven intervention demonstrated a significant reduction in health risks, such as sleep disturbances (OR 0.30) and depressive symptoms (OR 0.47). This proves that combining technology with solidarity can effectively address health inequalities in the gig economy.

## Presentation Details

### 1. Diagnosis: The Connectivity Paradox

**"Connected by algorithms, but isolated in life."**

- **Hyper-Competition**: Workers are connected to the platform to earn money but view each other as competitors rather than colleagues.
- **Tragic Reality**: The presentation highlights a tragic incident where a driver died in a shuttle bus after a shift. Fellow passengers (other drivers) did not notice the death until the end of the line because they did not know each other, illustrating the severe isolation in the field.
- **Structural Gap**:
  - Traditional health systems rely on fixed workplaces and employer-based management (Duty-based).
  - Gig workers have no fixed workplace and flow unilaterally through the system, leaving them with no health management support.

### 2. Direction for Solution

**From Unilateral Flow to a Circular Ecosystem**

1. **Innovation in Access**: Instead of workers visiting hospitals, the hospital system must visit the workers' "virtual workplaces"—the resting shelters and night shuttles (O2O Model).
2. **Data Integration**: Merging scattered National Health Insurance data with real-time biometric data (heart rate, steps) to visualize health risks.
3.  **Restoring Agency**: Transitioning from passive subjects to active participants. The "Participatory Research" model involved drivers acting as interviewers to survey their peers, leading to the formation of unions and self-help groups.

> **"Platform workers' health rights begin with providing knowledge and systems that allow them to care for themselves."**

### 3. Core Concept ① Data Integration & Structuring

- **Definition**: Integrating heterogeneous data sources based on a personal identifier (Person ID) to structure them into analyzable units.
- **Integrated Data Structure**:
  - **Clinical Data**: National health check-up results, on-site blood test results (Metabolic syndrome markers).
  - **Life-log**: 24-hour monitoring of heart rate, steps, and sleep patterns using wearable devices (Fitbit) over 2-4 weeks.
  - **Psychological/Environmental Data**: Surveys on insomnia (ISI), depression (CES-D), and work history.
- **Insight**: This approach reveals hidden correlations, such as the link between "continuity of night work" and "resting heart rate elevation," which standard check-ups often miss.

### 4. Core Concept ② Solidarity via Mutual Aid

- **Subject**: Moving beyond external research intervention to an internal system led by the "Platform Workers Mutual Aid Association" and Labor Unions.
- **System**:
  - **Financial Solidarity**: Securing funds from the Public Coexistence Solidarity Fund and financial institutions (e.g., Woori Bank) to support examination costs and ensure sustainability.
  - **Organizational Solidarity**: Embedding healthcare functions within self-help groups. "Health Leaders" encourage peers to participate in check-ups.

### 5. Core Concept ③ Accessibility & Return of Information

- **Philosophy**: "Medical results must be translated into the worker's language and returned to them."
- **Action Plan**:
  - **Automated Reporting**: Providing a personalized homepage (My Page) where scattered records are consolidated.
  - **Video Prescription**: Instead of difficult paper reports, doctors and nurses record personalized video messages explaining the results. Workers can watch these videos repeatedly during their commute or downtime.
- **Effect**: Ensuring the "Right to Know" leads to actual behavioral changes and better health management compliance.

### 6. Case Study 1: O2O Healthcare (Night Shuttle & Shelter)

**Transforming Transit Points into Field Clinics**

- **Context**: Designated drivers often congregate at specific "transfer points" or take shuttle buses late at night when public transport is unavailable.
- **Implementation**:
  - **Open Space Examination**: Transforming resting shelters and shuttle bus stops into temporary health check-up centers.
  - **Process**: Registration -> Physical Exam & Biospecimen sampling -> Doctor Consultation -> Post-management (Video upload).
- **Impact**: Removing temporal and spatial barriers by delivering medical services directly to the workers' path of movement.

### 7. Case Study 2: Intervention Efficacy

**The Power of Data-Driven Care**

- **Method**: Providing personalized health information videos and continuous notifications based on individual data.
- **Result**:
  - **Sleep Disturbance**: Reduced significantly in the intervention group (OR 0.30).
  - **Depressive Symptoms**: Showed a decreasing trend (OR 0.47).
- **Meaning**: Demonstrates that continuous monitoring and feedback, rather than just one-time financial support, lead to tangible health improvements.

### 8. Conclusion: From Isolation to Connection

1. **Technology**: Data integration and automated feedback systems (Video prescriptions).
2. **Space**: Delivering care to the field (Shuttles, Shelters).
3. **People**: Restoring the community through Mutual Aid Associations and Unions.

> **"Through the integration of data and solidarity, health management for platform workers transforms from a 'cost' into a sustainable 'asset'."**
